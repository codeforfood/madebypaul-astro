## Project Overview

JPL's software development teams relied on informal processes to learn about digital accessibility, relying often on best-effort attempts at detecting and remediating accessibility issues. There was a lack of clarity of who to go to with questions about digital accessibility or to find help with meeting accessibility compliance mandates.

This created a varied landscape, with different interpretations of what digital accessibility was and how it was achieved on our internal systems and content creation tools. There was no formal or instituationally sponsored training provided for digital accessibility.

## Challenges

- Lack of understanding on how to achieve compliance
- No central source for training or resources for accesibility testing and remediation
- No long-term support for digital accessibility on custom-built software
- Lack of advocacy for the disability community
